**Overwatch 2 ESP Menu** is a curated list of Overwatch 2 open-source tools:

- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — hero catalog, skin manager, overlay notes

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Hero Tracker — follow hero swaps in a practice layout
- Range Viewer — visualize ability ranges on the practice field
- Replay Notes — record and review practice runs
- Playback Speed — adjust practice playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Hero Catalog — browse heroes, skins, and highlight intros
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize cosmetic sets
- Outline Visibility — improved silhouette clarity in practice

### Aimbot
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any layout
- Camera Control — manually adjust the practice camera
- Movement Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the practice range
